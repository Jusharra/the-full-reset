Straight Talk

Questions guys actually ask

Is this actually discreet?+

Yes — evaluations happen online, and providers ship in plain, unmarked packaging. Nothing on the outside of the box indicates what's inside.

Am I guaranteed a prescription?+

No — and you shouldn't want a site that guarantees one. A licensed provider reviews your intake and only prescribes if it's medically appropriate for you.

Do you prescribe or ship anything?+

No. Reset Him is not a pharmacy or medical practice. The Daily Plan is handled directly by Hims or BlueChew's own licensed providers. The P-Shot and GAINSWave are performed by our independent partner clinic — book your spot here, and they'll take it from there.

What happens after I book the P-Shot or GAINSWave?+

You'll get an instant confirmation, and the clinic will contact you directly within 24–48 hours to lock in your exact appointment time.

Can I bundle this with hair restoration?+
