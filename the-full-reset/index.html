Straight Talk

Questions guys actually ask

Is The Full Reset a single payment?+

No — you still pay each provider directly at their standard rate (Hims/BlueChew, Keeps, and the PRP medspa). "The Full Reset" is the coordinated intake and priority booking wrapped around those services, not a repackaged price.

Do I have to do all three at once?+

You can stagger start dates, but the intake and PRP priority slot are reserved together — reach out on the Contact page and we'll sequence it around your schedule.

What if I only want one piece of it?+

Totally fine — check out Performance or Hair individually. The bundle is for guys who know they want both.

How does the application process work?+

Submit the form on our Contact page. Applications are reviewed weekly against this quarter's remaining PRP and concierge capacity — if there's a spot open for you, we follow up within one business day to confirm and schedule.
